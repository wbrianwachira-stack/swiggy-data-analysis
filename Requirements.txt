SWIGGY SALES ANALYSIS - PROJECT REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
The objective of this project is to perform a comprehensive data analysis of Swiggy’s sales performance to evaluate operational health and consumer behavior trends.

2. DATA SCOPE
- Dataset Size: 197,430 rows.
- Period: January 2025 to August 2025.
- Fields: State, City, Order Date, Restaurant Name, Location, Category, Dish Name, Price (INR).

3. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)
The analysis must calculate and display the following high-level metrics:
- Total Sales: Overall revenue generated from food orders.
- Total Orders: The absolute count of food orders received.
- Average Order Value (AOV): The average revenue generated per order.
- Average Rating: The mean customer satisfaction level across all restaurants.
- Ratings Count: Total number of customer reviews provided.

4. VISUALIZATION REQUIREMENTS (CHART BRD)
The project must include the following visual representations:
- Monthly Sales Trend: To monitor revenue fluctuations month-by-month.
- Daily Sales Trend: To highlight order variations across days of the week.
- Weekly Trend Analysis: To identify consistency or peak periods.
- Sales by Food Type: A comparison of revenue contribution between Veg and Non-Veg.
- Sales by State: A map-based visualization of revenue distribution.
- Top 5 Cities: A ranking of the leading cities contributing to total revenue.
- Quarterly Performance Summary: A combined view of Sales, Ratings, and Orders by Quarter.

5. TECHNICAL STACK
- Language: Python
- Libraries: Pandas (Data Handling), NumPy (Numerical Analysis), Matplotlib/Seaborn (Static Visualization), Plotly Express (Interactive Charts).
- Environment: Jupyter Notebook.

6. DELIVERABLES
- Cleaned and processed dataset.
- Interactive Python Notebook (.ipynb) with documented code.
- Stakeholder Presentation (PDF/PPT) summarizing insights.
